# **Brand Messaging Framework**

## Core Brand DNA

### The Elevator Pitch

For over a decade, Vancouver Startup Week has been the heartbeat of BC’s startup scene. We equip founders, dreamers, and doers with the tools, connections, and confidence to thrive—whether here in BC or on the global stage.

### Tone of Voice

#### **Action-Oriented**

We break the mold of the passive, listen-only conference. VSW is a hands-on, immersive experience designed to give you the tools you need, not just the theory.

#### **Authentic**

We capture the "Vancouver-vibe"—relaxed, not manicured or managed.

#### **Human**

We highlight the reality of running a business, celebrating the wins, losses, and lessons learned.

#### **Unintimidating**

Accessible, welcoming, and never overwhelming for those new to the community.

## Strategic Messaging Pillars

#### **Pillar 1: Connection is the Goal** 

We create a relaxed environment where the primary outcome is genuine human connection, not just transactional networking.

#### **Pillar 2: The Human Side of Business**

We don't just talk about product; we talk about the journey. We show the human side of launching and running a startup.

#### **Pillar 3: Homegrown Success**

BC has everything startups need to succeed. We highlight why everything you need is right here at home.

## Audience-Specific Messaging Matrix

Use this guide to tailor your value proposition for specific segments.

| Audience | Core Need / Motivator | Key Message Hook |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Startups (Early Stage)** | Finding tools and connections; Overcoming isolation through community. | "Don't build alone. Get 5 jam-packed days of tools, mentors, and peers facing the same challenges."  |
| **Later-Stage Founders** | Giving back; supporting the community that helped them. | “Celebrate your wins and the resilience that built them. Share the lessons that paved your way to success.”  |
| **Investors** | Efficient deal flow without the constant "pitch" pressure. | "The best of both worlds: Private investor-only insights \+ direct access to BC's newest innovators."  |
| **Multinationals / Corps** | Finding new clients/service users. | "Find your next customer. Connect with an active audience of 10,000+ entrepreneurs growing fast."  |
| **Government** | Economic growth and job creation. | "Meet the dreamers and doers whose passion will carry the province's economy forward." |

## Strategic Do's and Don'ts

* **DO:** Keep it accessible. Use language that is "unintimidating for new entrepreneurs".  
* **DO:** Highlight the ecosystem. Remind audiences that "VSW is one of the only times you'll see the entire BC startup community in one place".  
* **DON'T:** Be overly corporate or manicured. Keep the tone "authentic to the Vancouver-vibe".  
* **DON'T:** Focus only on "Pitching." Remind investors and founders that this is a space to "relax and reconnect," not just transact.
