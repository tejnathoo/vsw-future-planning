# LinkedIn Outreach — Objection Handling

**Usage note:** These responses are for private DM conversations. VSW does not reference other events by name in published or public-facing content.

---

## All Audiences

**Objection:** 'We're already going to Web Summit.'
**Response:** 'That's great — a lot of people are doing both, and they serve really different purposes. A global conference is about visibility and exposure. VSW is where the BC-specific relationships actually get built — the mentors, co-founders, investors, and partners who are right here and building alongside you year-round. One week, the whole ecosystem in one room. It's a different kind of value.'

---

## Startups (Early Stage)

**Objection:** 'We're too early. We're not ready for something like this yet.'
**Response:** 'VSW is specifically built for where you're at right now. Most people who say they weren't ready end up saying it was the best time to come — before the noise kicks in. You'll find co-founders, early mentors, and peers who are in the exact same stage. The earlier you show up, the more you get out of it.'

**Objection:** 'We can't afford it right now.'
**Response:** 'The All-Access Pass is priced to be accessible — it's one of the most affordable ways to spend a full week inside the BC startup ecosystem. And honestly, one good intro from the week tends to pay for it many times over. Happy to send you the details if budget is the main thing.'

**Objection:** 'We've been to startup events before and they're not really our thing.'
**Response:** 'That's fair — a lot of startup events feel like they're more about optics than actual value. VSW tends to be different because the whole ecosystem shows up, not just the people who want to be seen. You get candid conversations with founders who've been through it and aren't performing. Worth experiencing once and deciding for yourself.'

**Objection:** 'We're not based in Vancouver.'
**Response:** 'A lot of the founders who get the most out of VSW aren't local. The BC ecosystem is worth knowing regardless of where you're building — the investors, accelerators, and community are genuinely accessible here in a way that's harder to find in bigger markets. And it's five days, so the time commitment is contained.'

**Objection:** 'We're too busy right now.'
**Response:** 'Completely get it. The pass gives you flexibility — you don't have to be there all five days. Even two or three sessions and a couple of side events can make the week worth it. What would make it easiest to justify the time?'

---

## Later-Stage Founders

**Objection:** 'I've already got my network. Not sure what else I'd get out of it.'
**Response:** 'At your stage, VSW is less about building your own network and more about being a resource for the people who are a few years behind you. A lot of the later-stage founders who keep coming back say it's the most grounding week of their year — you remember what early looked like, and the conversations you have actually matter to someone. It's also just a good excuse to reconnect with people you haven't seen in a while.'

**Objection:** 'I don't have time to be there for a full week.'
**Response:** 'You don't have to do the whole week. A lot of later-stage founders drop in for a panel or a fireside and end up staying longer because the conversations are good. Even a half-day tends to be worth it. Is there a format — workshop, panel, dinner — that would be easiest to fit in?'

**Objection:** 'I've spoken at these kinds of events before and I'm not sure it's the best use of my time.'
**Response:** 'The ask isn't necessarily to speak — though if you're open to it, we'd love to have you. It's more about showing up and letting the conversations happen. Some of the most useful things that come out of VSW for people at your stage aren't on the schedule.'

---

## Investors

**Objection:** 'We get deal flow through other channels. We don't really need more events.'
**Response:** 'VSW isn't really a pitch event — that's actually what makes it useful. You get proximity to BC's early-stage companies in a low-pressure environment, before they're in active raise mode and pitching everywhere. Investor-only sessions keep it focused, and the side conversations tend to be more candid than a formal meeting. It's a different kind of signal.'

**Objection:** 'We don't invest in Canadian companies.'
**Response:** 'Fair enough — though a few investors who came to VSW not specifically looking at Canadian deals ended up finding companies worth a second look. BC's ecosystem is at a genuinely interesting moment, and the founders coming up here are building differently than what you typically see. Worth a conversation at minimum.'

**Objection:** 'We've attended before and the quality of startups wasn't what we were looking for.'
**Response:** 'Good to know — that feedback matters. A lot has changed in the last couple of years in terms of who's building here and what stage they're at. Happy to share more about what the 2026 cohort looked like if that's helpful context before you decide on 2027.'

## Multinationals / Corporates

**Objection:** 'We already sponsor other events. Our marketing budget is committed.'
**Response:** 'Totally understand. The distinction with VSW is the specificity of the audience — 5,050 BC founders and operators, all in one place for one week. If your product or service is relevant to that community, the ROI tends to be sharper than a broader conference where you're one booth among hundreds. Worth a conversation to see if the fit is actually there.'

**Objection:** 'Our target customer isn't really at startup events.'
**Response:** 'Depends on who you're targeting — VSW isn't just early-stage founders. You've got operators, investors, corporate teams, and ecosystem builders all in the same rooms. If any part of your customer base is in the BC startup and scaleup world, they're almost certainly at VSW. Happy to talk through whether the audience actually matches before you make a call.'

**Objection:** 'We're not sure what involvement would look like for us.'
**Response:** 'There's a range — from hosting a workshop or session, to activating at an event, to straight sponsorship. The format that works best tends to depend on what you're trying to get out of it. What's the main goal — brand awareness, lead gen, community relationships? That'd help me point you to the right fit.'

## Government

**Objection:** 'We already support other startup initiatives. I'm not sure how VSW fits.'
**Response:** 'VSW is one of the few events where you can get direct, unfiltered access to the founders and operators who are actually building here — not through a program lens, but in a room where they're being candid about what they need. If part of your mandate is understanding what's working and what isn't in the ecosystem, that access is hard to replicate.'

**Objection:** 'This isn't a priority for us right now.'
**Response:** 'Understood. If it ever becomes relevant — especially as you're thinking about what economic development looks like in the BC startup context — VSW is worth being in the room for. Happy to keep you on the list for 2027 info when it's out.'
