# VSW Differentiators — LinkedIn Outreach Intake Form

**Prompt:** What makes you different? Key differentiators, results, case studies, social proof.

---

**1. The whole ecosystem, one week.** VSW is the one time per year when the entire BC startup community — founders, investors, operators, accelerators, corporates, and students — is in the same room. No other event in the region creates that density. VSW 2026 brought together 5,050 attendees, 250+ speakers, and 85+ events across five days.

**2. Over a decade of community trust.** Most startup events chase the next hot thing. VSW has been the consistent, annual heartbeat of BC's startup scene for 10+ years. That longevity is the credential — it means the community keeps coming back on its own, not because of marketing.

**3. The BC model, not Silicon Valley cosplay.** VSW leans into what makes Vancouver distinct: sustainable growth over hype cycles, human-centered building, long-term thinking. As one founder who attended VSW 2026 put it: "Not replicating the Silicon Valley approach. Long-term, sustainable ventures — built with the right tools and aligned to the values and lifestyles of the founders. The BC model of entrepreneurship."

**4. Accessible by design.** VSW is explicitly welcoming to pre-idea and pre-seed founders, not just the ones who've already made it. That mix — napkin-sketch builders sitting next to serial founders — creates unusually honest conversations. Multiple attendees have called it "the first place I realized I wasn't building alone."

**5. External validation.** Canada Now (May 2026) covered VSW as "the foundation" of Vancouver's startup ecosystem, quoting 7 founders and operators on why the event's human-centered, grounded approach stands out even as global attention turns toward the city.
